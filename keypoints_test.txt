Artificial intelligence is transforming many industries by automating repetitive tasks and helping organizations analyze large amounts of data. Machine learning allows systems to learn patterns from existing data and make predictions without being explicitly programmed for every situation.

Natural language processing enables computers to understand and generate human language. It is widely used in chatbots, document summarization, translation, sentiment analysis, and information extraction.

AI systems are increasingly being combined with cloud computing to provide scalable services. However, organizations must also consider privacy, security, reliability, and responsible use when deploying AI applications.
